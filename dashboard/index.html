    Checklist

    Before you start

      Head count

      How many students are in the room?

      Background objects can look like faces for tens of seconds at a time, which no automatic test can tell apart from a student. You know how many people are here; the system then tracks only that many, keeping the nearest faces.

      After the exam

      Review the session

      The written report, a replay of any recorded session, and the snapshots saved when a student reached ALERT or FLAG. Nothing here decides anything: it is the record a person reviews.

      Session report

      A PDF to print, keep and hand over

      Findings, named patterns, evidence images and a timeline for every student, written to describe what was observed, never to accuse. One is also written automatically when the exam ends.

      Students

      Each student's own straight-ahead

      Calibration

      In the first seconds at the desk, the system learns how each student naturally sits. Everything afterwards is measured against that, so a student who sits slightly turned is never judged by someone else's posture.

      Does it work?

      Validation

      Clips recorded to show known behaviour are replayed through the real engine, exactly as a live exam would be, and each result is compared with what the clip was recorded to show. A match means the system behaved as intended on that footage. It is not proof of accuracy in general.

      What if…

      Try another setting on the same clips

      Re-scores every clip from the numbers saved during the last check, with one setting changed. Takes seconds and never touches a live exam. With a handful of clips from one person it shows the direction of a trade-off, not accuracy, and on its own it is never a reason to change a default.

      Recording

      Clips to record

      About two minutes each, in the exam seat, with the webcam the engine uses. Sit normally for the first ten seconds of every clip: that is when the engine learns your normal posture. Run each command from the project folder.
